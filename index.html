How would you like it done?

Our roast guide

We get asked for medium-rare a lot. Here's how our roast levels actually work.

Light

Not "rare"

Bright, fruity, tea-like. Best as a pour over or batch brew. Shows off where the bean came from.

Medium

Never "medium-rare"

Our house blend. Caramel, cocoa, a bit of stone fruit. Built for milk and for flat whites by the fire.

Dark

Not "well done"

Rich, chocolatey, low acidity. Roasted for people who like their coffee to taste like coffee.

About

Why "Beef Roasters"?

It started as a typo on a council form and we decided we liked it better than the name we'd picked. Since then we've had a steady stream of people walk in asking for brisket. We send them out with a flat white instead, and most of them come back.

We roast in small batches in the back of the shop, a few times a week, so the beans in your cup are never more than a couple of weeks off the roaster. Our house blend is designed for milk. The single origins rotate with whatever we're excited about.

The shop sits among the mountain ash and tree ferns of the Dandenong Ranges. Come in from a walk, sit by the fire and have a coffee. You won't find any beef.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

Do you sell steak?

No. We roast coffee, not beef. It's on the sign, the cups and this website.

Can I get my coffee medium-rare?

We can do you a medium roast. That's as close as we get. See the roast guide.

Is the coffee grass-fed?

The coffee plants are fed with sunshine, rain and mountain soil, so in a way, yes. Please don't ask the barista this before 9am.

Do you have any beef with other cafés?

None at all. The Ranges have plenty of good coffee and we're happy to be part of it.

Do you do food?

No. Coffee, breakfast tea and breakfast juice only. You're welcome to bring a pastry from down the road.

Do you have plant milks?

Yes: oat, soy and almond, for an extra $0.80.

Can I buy your beans wholesale for my café or office?

Yes. Send us a message through the contact form and pick "Wholesale beans".

Are dogs welcome?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome at the outdoor tables. Water bowl provided. No beef treats, obviously.
